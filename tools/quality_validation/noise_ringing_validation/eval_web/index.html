Both criteria required. Definitions are from NOISE_RINGING_RUBRIC.md.

Noise Freedom from grain / chroma noise

Visible sensor grain / luminance speckles or chroma (color) blotches that are not part of the real scene texture and that reduce clean tonal areas or subject readability. Typical places: shadows, under-exposed walls, night sky, smooth skin, flat painted surfaces.

    Ringing Artifacts Freedom from edge halos

    Light/dark halos, ghost ridges, or oscillatory fringes along high-contrast edges, usually from oversharpening or codec/compression edge ringing. Looks like a bright or dark outline that does not belong to the object.

      Select Noise and Ringing ratings (1–5).

      Confusion reminders (from rubric)
      • Random sand-like speckles → Noise, not Ringing.
      • Structured fabric/foliage weave → texture, not Noise.
      • 8×8 squares in flat areas → Blocking, not Ringing or Noise.
      • Bright/dark halo along an edge → Ringing.
      • Clean high-contrast edge without a companion ridge → not Ringing.
